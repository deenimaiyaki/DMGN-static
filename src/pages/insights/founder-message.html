A message from our founder

By Zahradeen Aliyu Maiyaki

Building DMGN: a vision of connection

When I started DMGN — Deeni Maiyaki Global Network — my dream was simple: to create a platform that connects communities, empowers individuals, and transforms ideas into impact. I believed that technology, creativity and collaboration could bridge gaps across cultures and industries.

Why DMGN exists

  • Empowerment — giving people tools to grow their businesses, skills and networks.
  • Innovation — using digital solutions to solve real-world challenges.
  • Community — building a global family where collaboration drives success.

Expanding our horizons

DMGN is more than digital networking. Today we work across travel, our Creative Hub, learning, and graffiti and wall art. We have also built out graphics and video editing to help businesses and creators tell their stories visually.

Health meets technology

I have been using Sweatcoin, an app that rewards you for walking. It aligns with what DMGN is for: empowering people to live healthier, more connected lives. Every step you take can lead to opportunity.

Looking ahead

DMGN is still growing, but the mission is clear: to connect, empower and inspire. As founder, I am committed to making DMGN a trusted hub for innovation, creativity and community impact.
