Sweatcoin + DMGN Lifestyle

Walk into opportunity

At DMGN we believe every step can lead to growth. Sweatcoin motivates you to stay active by turning your steps into a digital currency. With DMGN, those sweatcoins become part of a bigger journey — connecting health, travel and creativity.

Travel with your steps

DMGN Travel lets you explore the world. Imagine walking daily, earning sweatcoins, and redeeming them against trips and experiences. Your fitness fuels your adventures.

Creativity powered by movement

Through DMGN Creative Hub, sweatcoins can support artists, creators and innovators — helping fund creativity and storytelling across communities.

Community impact

With DMGN Connect, walking isn't just personal — it's collective. Rewards can be shared into community projects, mural work and local initiatives that beautify and inspire.

Join the movement

Walking is more than exercise — it's a lifestyle. With Sweatcoin and DMGN, your steps can unlock travel, creativity and community impact.
