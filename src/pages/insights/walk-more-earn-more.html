Walk more, earn more with Sweatcoin

Why Sweatcoin?

At DMGN we believe in empowering communities through technology and lifestyle innovation. Sweatcoin is a free app that rewards you for walking, turning your steps into a digital currency called sweatcoins. It's a motivating way to stay active while earning rewards.

How it works

  • Step tracking — the app counts your outdoor steps.
  • Earn sweatcoins — every step adds up to digital currency.
  • Redeem rewards — use sweatcoins for products, services or offers.

Why join through DMGN?

Joining through DMGN puts you inside a community that values health, creativity and growth. Your steps don't just benefit you — they connect you to opportunities across the network.
